# Tessel: strategy 2026 to 2028

Tessel is a fictional company. It sells payment and invoicing software to small independent retailers in Europe: bakeries, bike shops, hair salons. It has about 450 staff and 38,000 merchants, most of them in the Netherlands, Belgium and the United Kingdom.

Growth at home has slowed, and too many new merchants leave within a year. For the next three years we concentrate on five priorities and rule out three things.

## Priorities

1. Win Germany and France: by the end of 2027, half of all new merchants should come from these two countries.
2. Make payments dependable: card and online payments must be available 99.95% of the time, and no merchant should lose a sale because of us.
3. Let merchants help themselves: a new merchant should be able to sign up, set up and take a first payment without talking to our staff.
4. Grow through accountants and resellers: partners who already advise small retailers should bring us at least a third of new merchants.
5. Keep the merchants we have: reduce the share of merchants who leave within their first year from 24% to 12%.

## What we will not do

1. We will not build features for a single customer, however large the contract.
2. We will not sell outside Europe.
3. We will not design or manufacture our own payment hardware.
